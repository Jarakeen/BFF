 Critical chance is capped at 100% (657 crit rating-> 3% crit chance), and critical damage is capped at +125%. Shadow is not meta because there are many sources of critical damage boost but there are not so many sources of critical rating boost.

Full details:

You start at a base amount of +50% critical damage, so you only need another +75% to reach cap. Medium armor gives 2% each, most meta trial PvE dps builds will run 4-7 pieces of Medium armor, even when built for mag, adding ~10%. Dungeon setups might run less for more pen, team comp dependent. Next, class passives - these can add around another 10%, depending on class. Some may not add any. Examples: Templar's Piercing Spear adding 10% or Arcanist's Fated Fortune adding 12%. Next, Minor Force - this is an expected buff to have on DPS, adding another 10%. Add CP stars in, for another 8-23% (Fighting Finesse, Backstabber - in my experience, backstabber is not used that often). These are more often used on dungeon builds especially mag dungeon builds, trial groups can bring in lots of additional buffs. Add Khajiit racial passive in if you are one, +12%.
Total: 50 + 10 + 10 + 10 + 8 = +88% crit damage, or +100% if khajiit.

Finally, there are group Buffs/Debuffs that are expected in a somewhat organized group:
Major Force: +20% - this is not expected to have at all times, but will be on for burst phases of fight.

Minor Brittle: +10% - this is expected to be on pretty much all the time on main targets.

Lucent Echoes: +11% - expected at all times in a trial group, some dungeon groups won't have this

Other buffs to critical damage:

Elemental catalyst: +15% - not often seen anymore with Lucent Echoes' release, but it does stack with the rest. Sometimes seen in dungeons on a support DPS paired with Master Architect, replacing the healer.

Major Brittle: +20% - only source in the game is the Nunatak PvP monster set, it is vanishingly rare to actually see used in PvE, but I have seen it used before.

Total Critical damage buffs from a typical group: +109% most of the time, +129% bursts.

These buffs easily push critical damage to cap and over. Shadow simply has no space to actually apply.
