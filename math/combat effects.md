
Burning
The Burning status can be applied by dealing Flame Damage to a target. When you apply the Burning status effect on an enemy, they take flame damage over time based on your Max Magicka and Spell Damage. Burning effect lasts 4 seconds and ticks every 2 seconds.

Chilled
The Chilled status can be applied by dealing Frost Damage to a target. A Chilled enemy takes instant frost damage and suffers from Minor Maim debuff for 4 seconds, reducing their damage by 5%. If you have an ice staff as your active weapon at the time it is applied, it will also apply Minor Brittle, increasing their critical damage taken.

Concussion
The Concussion status can be applied by dealing Shock Damage to a target. A Concussed enemy takes instant shock damage, increased by 15% if Concussion was already recently applied, and suffers from Minor Vulnerability for 4 seconds, increasing their damage taken by 5%.

Poisoned
The Poisoned status can be applied by dealing Poison Damage to a target. When you apply poisoned status effect on an enemy, they take Poison damage over time, increased by up to 100% based on their missing Health. Poisoned effect lasts 4 seconds and ticks every 2 seconds.

Diseased
The Diseased status can be applied by dealing Disease Damage to a target. A Diseased enemy takes instant direct disease damage that splashes in a 6 meter radius to enemies, up to once every 4 seconds. The enemy also suffers from Minor Defile debuff for 4 seconds, decreasing their healing received and damage shield strength by 6%.

Hemorrhaging
The Hemorrhaging status can be applied by dealing Bleed Damage to a target. When you apply Hemorrhaging status effect on an enemy, they take bleed damage over time, stacking up to 3 times. Each stack increases the damage by 25%. Hemorrhaging effect lasts 4 seconds and ticks every 2 seconds.

Sundered
The Sundered status can be applied by dealing Physical Damage to a target. A Sundered enemy takes instant physical damage and suffers from Minor Breach debuff for 4 seconds, decreasing their physical and spell resistance by 2974. Applying Sundered will also increase your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 100 for 4 seconds.

Overcharged
The Overcharged status can be applied by dealing Magic Damage to a target. An Overcharged enemy takes instant magic damage and restores 65 Magicka to the caster. They also suffer from Minor Magickasteal for 4 seconds, which restores 168 magicka per second to players dealing damage to them.

Other Effects
These are common negative effects enemies can apply to players in Trials and Dungeons. These cannot be removed with skills that say "remove negative effects"

Hindered
Healing your missing health is prevented for 12 seconds or until 39760 points of healing are received.

Rattled
You are shaken up for 12 seconds, reduces your damage done by 70% and increasing your damage taken by 40%.

Devitalized
You are weakened for 8 seconds, Reduces your Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance by 60%, reduces damage shields by 30% and increases damage taken increased by 30%.
