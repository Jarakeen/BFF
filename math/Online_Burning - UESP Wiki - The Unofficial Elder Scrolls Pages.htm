​ Burning is a debilitating effect that can be applied by doing fire damage. It deals additional Flame Damage over 4 seconds and makes targets more vulnerable to certain subsequent effects. For example, Wall of Fire deals additional damage to Burning enemies. This status effect can be removed with a Cleansing effect.
