# FoundryDock: Quick Start – Build a Team

This is the **fast path** for getting a team into FoundryDock and turning it into an actual raid plan.

You do **not** need to fill out every detail before you start. Get the people into seats first, then refine the plan.

---

## 1. Pick or Create Your Team

Go to **Roster → Teams**.

Create a new team or open one you already have.

A team is your **general group of players**. It is not tied to one particular trial or boss yet.

**For a trial team:**

* Choose **12 players**
* Use **Recruit** for any open seats
* Don't worry about builds or assignments yet

📸 **Picture:** Roster / Teams page
*Caption: Start here. Give the group a name and get twelve chairs filled.*

---

## 2. Put People in Their Seats

Go to the **Roster / Players** area and make sure the players you want are available.

Your basic raid seats are:

* **Tank 1**
* **Tank 2**
* **Healer 1**
* **Healer 2**
* **DD 1–8**

Assign each player to the appropriate seat.

If somebody hasn't been recruited yet, leave **Recruit** in that position. Recruit placeholders are just placeholders and aren't real saved players.

📸 **Picture:** Roster showing Tank / Healer / DD seats
*Caption: Chairs first. FoundryDock can worry about the frightening amount of ESO math afterward.*

---

## 3. Create a Raid Plan

Open **Raid Plan**.

Choose:

* Your **team**
* The **trial**
* The specific plan you want to work on

For example:

> Performance Mode → Sunspire → Godslayer

The Raid Plan is where the generic team becomes the group you're actually taking into specific content.

You can change classes here if needed, including for Recruit seats.

Hit **Save** before leaving the page.

📸 **Picture:** Raid Plan top card with team/trial selectors
*Caption: A Team is the people. A Raid Plan is what those people are doing tonight.*

---

## 4. Give Everyone Their Jobs

Go to **Assignments**.

This is where you tell FoundryDock **who is responsible for what**.

Examples:

* Major Courage
* Major Slayer
* Crusher
* Alkosh
* Powerful Assault
* Roaring Opportunist
* Elemental Catalyst
* Z'en
* Specific mechanics or utility jobs

Assignments matter because FoundryDock uses them when deciding what the group already has covered.

So if your werewolf DD is providing **Major Courage**, assign it here rather than making the optimizer invent another source.

📸 **Picture:** Assignments page
*Caption: Tell the app what each person is already responsible for before asking it to solve the rest.*

---

## 5. Open Comp Maker

Now go to **Comp Maker**.

Load your Raid Plan using **Get Players**.

Your twelve seats should appear with their:

* Player
* Class
* Role
* Existing assignments
* Planned gear where applicable

You can manually lock in anything you already know.

For example:

> Healer 1 = Roaring Opportunist + Jorvuld's Guidance
> Tank 1 = Turning Tide
> DD 3 = Z'en + Alkosh

You don't have to know the entire composition beforehand.

📸 **Picture:** Comp Maker with a loaded team
*Caption: Lock the things you know. Let the app help with the things you don't.*

---

## 6. Let FoundryDock Fill the Gaps

Once your fixed choices are entered, use the optimizer / team planning tools.

FoundryDock looks at:

* Trial requirements
* Existing player assignments
* Classes
* Roles
* Gear you've already locked
* Raid-wide buffs and debuffs already covered
* Buffs and debuffs still missing

The goal isn't to give twelve people twelve individually "best" builds.

The goal is to create **one team whose builds work together**.

That distinction is why this app has consumed an unreasonable amount of our lives.

---

## 7. Check Team Health / Coverage

Look at **Team Health** and **Coverage**.

You want FoundryDock to distinguish between:

**Covered**
Someone in the current plan is already providing it.

**Needs Attention**
The group still needs a source.

Click or hover over coverage details when available to see **who is providing each buff/debuff**.

Don't automatically assign another set just because you recognize its name. Check whether the effect is already being supplied another way.

📸 **Picture:** Team Health / Coverage panel
*Caption: Green means somebody has the job. Missing coverage means the group still needs an answer.*

---

## 8. Save the Plan

When the composition looks right, hit **Save**.

That preserves the plan so the rest of FoundryDock can use it for:

* Builds
* Coverage
* Raid readiness
* Raid leading tools
* Eventually Live Raid

---

# The Short Version

**Team → Raid Plan → Roles → Assignments → Comp Maker → Coverage → Builds**

Or, in ordinary human language:

**Who is coming → where they sit → what they're responsible for → what gear the group needs.**

You do **not** need perfect individual builds before starting the team.

Build the **raid composition first**, then finish the individual builds around it.

---

## Example

You're building a 12-person Sunspire team.

You already know:

* Healer 1 is running **RoJo**
* Healer 2 is running **SPC**
* Tank 1 has **Turning Tide**
* One DD is providing **Z'en**
* Another player is your werewolf and supplies **Major Courage**

Enter those known responsibilities first.

Then let FoundryDock identify what the raid is still missing rather than assigning duplicate sources.

That is the whole idea:

> **Tell FoundryDock what you already know. Let it help solve what you don't.**
